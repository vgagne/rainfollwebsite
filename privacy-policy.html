6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic Site functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4, used to understand Site usage
  • Marketing cookies: Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel and TikTok Pixel, used to measure ad performance and track campaign performance via UTM parameters

For visitors outside the United States, we display a cookie consent banner on your first visit, and non-essential cookies (analytics and marketing) are only activated after you provide consent; you may withdraw your consent or manage cookie preferences at any time through the cookie settings accessible on our Site. For visitors we geolocate to the United States, analytics and advertising tools activate automatically without a prior consent banner, consistent with applicable U.S. requirements; you may still opt out at the browser level (e.g., ad blockers, cookie settings) or by contacting us.

For more information about how these providers use your data, see: https://policies.google.com/privacy (Google), facebook.com/privacy/policy (Meta), and tiktok.com/legal/page/us/privacy-policy (TikTok)

6.1 Server-to-Server Conversion Measurement

In addition to browser-based pixels, we use server-to-server conversion measurement tools — Meta's Conversions API and TikTok's Events API — to help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools transmit hashed, non-reversible identifiers (such as a one-way cryptographic hash of your email address) rather than plain-text personal information, and are subject to the same activation rules described above: for visitors outside the United States, they only run once you have provided cookie consent; for visitors geolocated to the United States, they run automatically alongside the corresponding browser pixel.
