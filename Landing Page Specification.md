# Landing Page Specification — Shower Product (Validation Stage)

## Objective
Create a **high-converting, single-product landing page** designed to validate demand from paid traffic (TikTok/Instagram ads).

This page is NOT a full brand site. It is a **conversion-focused page** optimized for:
- Fast understanding (within 3–5 seconds)
- Clear product differentiation
- Strong visual demonstration
- Immediate action (buy or join waitlist)

---

# Core Principles (Must Follow)

- Visual > Text
- Show > Explain
- Fast > Pretty
- Clear > Clever

Users should understand:
1. What this is
2. Why it's better
3. Why they should care

...within seconds.

---

# Page Structure

## 1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)

### Goal:
Immediately communicate value and stop bounce.

### Content:
- **Headline (primary)**
  - "Turn Your Shower Into a Luxury Rainfall Experience in Minutes"

- **Subheadline**
  - "No drilling. No plumber. Use your entire shower space."

- **Hero Video (MANDATORY)**
  - Autoplay, muted, loop
  - Shows:
    - Product running
    - Dual water flow
    - Movement across shower
    - Person comfortably using it

- **Primary CTA Button**
  - "Get Yours" OR "Reserve Yours"

---

## 2. Problem Section (Relatability)

### Goal:
Make user feel understood immediately

### Format:
Short + visual

### Content:
- Visuals:
  - Extension arm setup (awkward)
  - Cramped positioning
  - Turning shower on and getting hit with water

- Text (short bullets):
  - "Stuck against the wall"
  - "Awkward positioning"
  - "Cheap-looking setups"
  - "No flexibility"

---

## 3. Solution Section (Product Intro)

### Goal:
Show how your product solves the problem

### Content:
- Short headline:
  - "This Changes Everything"

- Video or GIF:
  - Sliding/adjustable movement
  - Dual heads working
  - Full coverage

- Supporting bullets:
  - "Move your shower anywhere"
  - "Full-body coverage"
  - "Designed for real use"

---

## 4. Key Benefits (Not Features)

### Goal:
Translate product into outcomes

### Format:
3–4 blocks max

### Example:

#### Use Your Whole Shower
Stop being stuck in one spot

#### Install in Minutes
No tools. No permanent changes

#### Built for Real Comfort
Adjustable, flexible, intuitive

#### Feels Like a Luxury Upgrade
Without the renovation cost

---

## 5. How It Works (Very Simple)

### Goal:
Remove friction and doubt

### Format:
3 steps max

1. Attach to your existing shower
2. Adjust to your space
3. Turn on and enjoy

Include simple visuals/icons if possible

---

## 6. Demo Section (Proof)

### Goal:
Reinforce belief

### Content:
- Multiple short looping clips:
  - Water pressure close-up
  - Full system running
  - Movement in action
  - Person using comfortably

---

## 7. Trust / Risk Reduction

### If no reviews yet:
Use statements, not fake testimonials

- "Designed for real-world use"
- "Tested for durability and performance"
- "Built with high-quality materials"

Optional:
- Early user quotes if available

---

## 8. Final CTA Section

### Goal:
Capture action

- Headline:
  - "Upgrade Your Shower Today"

- CTA Button:
  - "Get Yours Now"

- Optional urgency:
  - "Limited first production run"

---

# Technical Requirements

## Performance
- Fast load time (<3 seconds)
- Mobile-first design (critical)
- Video optimized for fast loading

---

## UX Behavior

- Sticky CTA button (mobile)
- Minimal scrolling friction
- No distractions (no menus, no links out)

---

## Tracking (Minimum Required)

### Install:
- TikTok Pixel
- Meta Pixel (if using Instagram/Facebook)

### Track:
- Page View
- Button Click (CTA)
- Purchase or Email Submit (if applicable)

---

# Copy Guidelines

- Short sentences
- No technical jargon
- No long paragraphs
- Focus on outcomes, not engineering

---

# What NOT to Include

- Long brand story
- Patent discussion
- Technical specs (unless very simplified)
- Overly polished marketing language
- Multiple products

---

# Alignment With Ads

Your ads focus on:
- Extension arm problems
- Shower usability
- Easy install
- Visual transformation

Your landing page MUST:
- Continue the same message
- Show the same scenarios
- Not introduce new confusing ideas

---

# Success Criteria

You know the page is working if:
- People stay on page
- People click CTA
- Messaging feels consistent with ads

If ads get clicks but no conversion:
→ landing page mismatch or weak clarity

---

# Final Instruction

This page is a **test tool**, not a finished asset.

Do not overbuild.

Get it live fast, drive traffic, learn, and iterate.
